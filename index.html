What is the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO optimizes for ranking in traditional search results. GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) and AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) optimize for being cited inside AI-generated answers instead of, or in addition to, a ranked link.

Why do some sites get quoted by ChatGPT or Google's AI Overviews and others don't?

Mostly structure. Clear, answer-first content, explicit schema.org/JSON-LD markup, and technical SEO that lets both a search crawler and an AI system parse the page cleanly. Sites that bury the answer inside marketing copy get skipped, even when the information is technically on the page.
